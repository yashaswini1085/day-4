Food waste reduction platform

A large amount of food is wasted every day by households, restaurants, hotels, canteens, and events, while many people still struggle to get sufficient food. There is no efficient platform to connect surplus food with people or organizations that can use it. This project aims to develop a Food Waste Reduction Platform that helps identify, manage, and redistribute surplus food to reduce food wastage and support people in need.
