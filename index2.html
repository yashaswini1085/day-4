Established in 2009 by educationist Sri A. Varada Reddy garu, SRITW was founded with a clear vision: to empower women in science and technology and transform them into innovators, leaders and entrepreneurs.

Today, our women’s engineering college pairs rigorous learning with a culture of invention, confidence and meaningful opportunity.
